# Humanization Game Plan: Seminar Thesis

Based on the core diagnosis and rule sets provided by the user's friend, this game plan outlines the steps for a systematic "humanization" of the seminar thesis. 

The goal is to move from a "textbook/AI-narrated" style to a "researcher/authorial" voice, while preserving every technical concept, explanation, and example.

---

## Part 1: Strategic Priorities

### 1. Structural Surgery (Rule Set 1, 7, 8)
*   **Remove "Scaffolding":** Delete all chapter previews (Type A), section previews (Type B), and transition bridges (Type C). These add word count without adding technical value.
*   **Fix Repetition:** The "open-source blind spot" is explained 4 times. I will consolidate this into one strong explanation in Chapter 1/4 and use brief forward/backward references elsewhere.
*   **Rewrite Priority Sections:**
    *   **Abstract:** Move from an executive summary to a continuous technical argument.
    *   **Section 7.1:** Change the "profound insight" framing to a direct technical claim.
    *   **Section 6.2:** Jump straight to the PID filtering mechanism without the "here is how it works in practice" setup.

### 2. Stylistic Refinement (Rule Set 2, 4, 6)
*   **Intensifier Sweep:** Search for "completely", "absolutely", "massive", "critical", "simply", etc., and reduce their frequency by 70%.
*   **Researcher Framing:** Replace "To make this concrete, imagine..." with direct technical scenarios. Introduce "first-person friction" (hedging or pushing back on paper claims) to show active engagement with the source material.
*   **Connector Vocabulary:** Replace "However", "Furthermore", and "Additionally" with semicolons, appositives, and direct syntactic connections.

### 3. Sentence & List Dynamics (Rule Set 3, 5)
*   **Vary Sentence Length:** Ensure every paragraph has at least one short (<12 words) and one long (>45 words) sentence. Break the "AI rhythm" of uniform 20-35 word sentences.
*   **Irregular Lists:** Break the perfect parallelism in bulleted lists. Add a caveat or a detailed trailing explanation to one item in each list.
